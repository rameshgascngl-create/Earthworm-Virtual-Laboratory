# Native scientific audit — Phase 1

Target: `native/v2.0.0-alpha1`  
Organism model: *Metaphire posthuma* (Vaillant, 1868), historically taught as *Pheretima posthuma*.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Species-specific modern redescription** — Bantaowong et al. (2011), *Tropical Natural History* 11(1):55–69, redescription of *Metaphire posthuma*.
2. **Primary system-specific anatomy** — K. N. Bahl (1919), *Journal of Cell Science*, “On a New Type of Nephridia found in Indian Earthworms of the Genus Pheretima”, including *Pheretima posthuma*.
3. **Traditional Indian practical convention** — retained only when the modern species redescription does not enumerate the same detail; such material must be labelled as teaching convention rather than presented as modern redescription evidence.

## Phase-1 findings

### External morphology — CORE SPECIES CHARACTERS VERIFIED

Species-specific redescription supports:
- epilobous prostomium;
- first dorsal pore 12/13;
- annular clitellum XIV–XVI;
- setae retained ventrally on the clitellum;
- female pore single, midventral XIV;
- four pairs of spermathecal pores 5/6–8/9;
- male pores paired on XVIII;
- paired genital markings on XVII and XIX.

**Correction applied:** removed the assessment claim that setae are absent from clitellar segments. The native external schematic now visibly retains ventral clitellar setae and separates genital markings from the male pores.

### Digestive system — SPECIES SEGMENT DATA VERIFIED

Published *M. posthuma* accounts agree on the sequence of a muscular gizzard before the intestine but differ in exact gizzard segment placement. The 2011 Thai redescription reports IX–X, while Indian material and classic practical accounts report VIII. The intestine begins at XV. Published accounts consistently place the origin of the paired simple intestinal caeca in XXVII, while their anterior reach varies approximately between XXIV and XXV.

**Corrections applied:**
- removed the false implication that either VIII or IX–X is universal for every *M. posthuma* specimen;
- redefined the traditional "stomach" as the post-gizzard, pre-intestinal teaching region so it cannot overlap the gizzard after a source-specific segment choice;
- caecal origin corrected to XXVII while anterior extent is explicitly treated as variable;
- assessment answer tests the robust XXVII caecal origin rather than a variable endpoint.

### Circulatory system — PARTIAL EVIDENCE / PROVENANCE CAUTION

The modern species redescription directly states that the **last hearts are in XIII**.

The complete VII / IX / XII / XIII four-pair map comes from the traditional Indian practical account. It remains educationally useful but is now explicitly labelled as a teaching convention rather than attributed to the modern redescription.

**Correction applied:** the assessment now tests the directly verified species-level point (last hearts in XIII), not the provenance-sensitive full four-pair map.

### Excretory system — PRIMARY ANATOMICAL SUPPORT

Bahl's primary account of *Pheretima posthuma* supports:
- three distinct nephridial categories: septal, pharyngeal and integumentary;
- pharyngeal nephridia as paired tufts in IV–VI;
- pharyngeal and septal enteronephric drainage;
- integumentary nephridia as minute separate units opening independently to the exterior;
- septal nephridia connected to segmental enteronephric duct systems.

The current native dataset is consistent with these core relationships.

**Status:** text PASS; visual plate remains QA OPEN for positional clarity.

### Reproductive system — CORE SPECIES CHARACTERS VERIFIED

Species-specific redescription supports:
- paired testis sacs in X and XI;
- paired seminal vesicles in XI and XII;
- paired ovaries in XIII;
- four pairs of spermathecae in VI–IX;
- racemose paired prostate glands centred on XVIII and extending XVII–XX in the cited material.

**Correction applied:** native schematic now draws four **pairs** of spermathecae, two pairs of testes, two pairs of seminal vesicles, paired ovaries, paired vasa deferentia and paired prostate fields.

### Transverse section — TARGETED STRUCTURAL CORRECTION

The native plate now explicitly shows:
- dorsal vessel on the gut;
- typhlosole as an inward intestinal fold rather than a separate tube;
- ventral vessel above the ventral nerve cord;
- subneural vessel below the ventral nerve cord.

**Status:** structural relationship corrected; full histological/visual acceptance remains QA OPEN.

## Explicit unresolved items

- Published *M. posthuma* specimens show genuine variation in gizzard placement and male-pore surface morphology; the app must preserve specimen/source awareness rather than manufacture one universal morphology.
- The complete segment-by-segment four-heart map remains a **curriculum convention with provenance warning**, not a direct claim from the cited modern redescription.
- Nephridial visual scale and exact duct-course representation remain schematic.
- Canvas plates are not segment-proportional unless explicitly stated.
- Tamil terminology requires a separate editorial parity pass.
- No plate is production-accepted until physical-device screenshot review confirms visibility, touch endpoints and absence of clipping.

## Release consequence

Scientific audit Phase 1 does **not** authorize production release. It authorizes the next controlled pass: visual endpoint verification and bilingual terminology review, followed by physical-device QA.

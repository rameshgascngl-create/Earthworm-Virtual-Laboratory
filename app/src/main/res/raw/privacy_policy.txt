Privacy Policy — Earthworm Virtual Laboratory

Effective date: 28 September 2026
App: Earthworm Virtual Laboratory
Package: in.ramesh.zoology.earthwormlab
Developer / Institution: Department of Zoology, Government Arts and Science College, Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu, India
Contact: rameshgascngl@gmail.com

OVERVIEW

Earthworm Virtual Laboratory is an offline educational application for undergraduate Zoology teaching. The app is designed to work without a user account, advertising, analytics, or remote data collection.

INFORMATION COLLECTED

The app does not ask for or collect a student's name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera data, microphone recordings, photos, files, or advertising identifier.

The Android application requests no INTERNET permission and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

INFORMATION STORED ON THE DEVICE

The app may keep limited learning-state and preference information locally on the device so that the learning experience can continue between sessions. Depending on the features used, this may include:
• language preference;
• the last selected anatomical system or structure;
• locally stored guided-learning or assessment state.

This information stays on the device and is not transmitted to the developer or college by the app. Android cloud backup is disabled.

SPEECH

Narration uses an Android text-to-speech voice installed on the device. The app selects voices that Android reports as not requiring a network connection. The application does not send narration text to an online speech service.

EXTERNAL LINKS

Scientific reference links and the optional public web copy of this Privacy Policy open only after a user action in the device's external browser. The app itself does not load remote web content.

ADVERTISING AND ANALYTICS

The app contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. The developer does not sell or share student information with advertisers.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The app does not create user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or students. Learning progress remains on the device.

SECURITY AND RETENTION

The app is designed as an offline teaching resource with local-only progress storage. Data remains on the device until app data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future release changes permissions, network behaviour, analytics, account systems, or data handling, this policy will be updated before that release is distributed.

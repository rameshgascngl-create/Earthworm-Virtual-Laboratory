# Privacy Policy — Earthworm Virtual Laboratory

**Effective date:** 28 September 2026  
**App:** Earthworm Virtual Laboratory  
**Package:** `in.ramesh.zoology.earthwormlab`  
**Developer / Institution:** Department of Zoology, Government Arts and Science College, Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu, India  
**Contact:** rameshgascngl@gmail.com

## Overview

Earthworm Virtual Laboratory is an offline educational application for undergraduate Zoology teaching. The app is designed to work without a user account, advertising, analytics, or remote data collection.

## Information collected

The app does **not** ask for or collect a student's name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera data, microphone recordings, photos, files, or advertising identifier.

The Android application requests **no INTERNET permission** and includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

## Information stored on the device

The app may keep limited learning-state and preference information locally on the device so that the learning experience can continue between sessions. Depending on the features used, this may include:

- language preference;
- the last selected anatomical system or structure;
- locally stored assessment score/state fields, where used; and
- a local privacy-policy acknowledgement state.

This information is not transmitted by the app to the developer, college, advertisers, or analytics services.

Android cloud backup is disabled for this application. Local app data normally remains until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the app.

## Speech

Narration uses Android text-to-speech voices installed on the device. The app selects voices that Android reports as not requiring a network connection. The application does not send narration text to an online speech service.

## External links

When the user chooses the Privacy Policy or another external reference, the requested address is opened in the device's external web browser. The Earthworm Virtual Laboratory app itself does not load remote web content because it has no INTERNET permission. The external browser and visited websites have their own privacy practices.

## Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. The developer does not sell or share student information with advertisers.

## Children and students

The app does not create user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or students. Learning-state information remains on the device.

## Security and retention

The app is designed as an offline teaching resource with local-only storage. Local app data remains on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the application.

## Changes to this policy

If a future release changes the permissions, network behaviour, analytics, account system, or data handling of the application, this policy will be updated before that release is distributed.
